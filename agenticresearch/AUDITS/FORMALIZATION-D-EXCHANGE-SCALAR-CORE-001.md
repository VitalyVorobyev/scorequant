# Formal statement audit of the D-exchange scalar core

**Claim:** `D-EXCHANGE-SCALAR-CORE`  
**Date:** 31 August 2026  
**Result:** the Lean specification matches exactly the scalar subclaim used in D5; it does not formalize the full finite exchange-to-Voronoi theorem

## Informal source

The source is D5 in `KNOWN_RESULTS/04-d-optimality.md`, independently checked
in `AUDITS/AUDIT-D-EXCHANGE-VORONOI-001.md` sections 7--9. After the rank-two
matrix and determinant reductions, write

\[
q_\delta=q_{aa}+q_{bb}-2q_{ab},\qquad
E=\alpha q_{aa}-\beta q_{bb}
-\alpha\beta(q_{aa}q_{bb}-q_{ab}^2).
\]

The scalar step uses (q_{bb}\le q_{aa}), nonnegative quadratic products,
the leverage inequality

\[
0\le q_\delta\le 1/W_a+1/W_b,
\]

and positive relocation coefficients generated by

\[
0<w<W_a,\quad W_b>0,\quad
\alpha=\frac{wW_a}{W_a-w},\quad
\beta=\frac{wW_b}{W_b+w}.
\]

cell-mass identity rather than taking it as an assumption.

## Conclusion correspondence

`scalarExchangeStrengthenedLowerBound` proves

\[
E\ge \frac{\alpha\beta}{4}
\left(q_\delta^2+(q_{aa}-q_{bb})^2\right).
\]

`scalarExchangeLowerBound` drops the nonnegative second square, and
`scalarExchangeExcessPositive` proves strict positivity when (q_\delta>0).
These are exactly the scalar inequalities used in D5.

## Explicit non-coverage

Lean does not yet check:

- the rank-two information update or determinant lemma;
- that the quadratic products arise from one common positive-definite metric;
- logarithm monotonicity and the stated bound on \(\Delta F_D\);
- why distinct centroids give \(q_\delta>0\);
- the singleton, merged-duplicate, feasibility, or zero-tolerance arguments;
- the final implication from exchange stability to strict Voronoi geometry;
- any Python/JAX implementation.

Accordingly, only `D-EXCHANGE-SCALAR-CORE` carries `formal_proof` metadata.
The broader `D-EXCHANGE-VIOLATION-LOWER-BOUND` and
`D-EXCHANGE-IMPLIES-VORONOI` claims remain informally proved and independently
audited but are not described as machine-checked.
