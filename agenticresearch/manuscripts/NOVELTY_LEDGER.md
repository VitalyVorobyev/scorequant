**Purpose.** One row per central statement of manuscript v8 (26 Aug 2026) and per registry
finding proved since v8, so that the v9 draft (session S5) can be written from this table and the
registry without re-deriving or re-attributing anything. The registry is canonical: resolve any
claim id with `python py/registry.py show <ID> --deps --proof`. This ledger records labels and
attribution; it proves nothing.

**Novelty vocabulary.** `known` — literature/bridge status, or `literature_search_status:
prior_art_found`. `direct corollary` — follows from known results by routine algebra.
`adaptation` — a known technique transferred to the score-space Fisher setting with real but
modest changes. `apparently new` — project-proved or counterexample with a recorded
`search_gap`; a search gap is not a novelty proof (`KNOWN_RESULTS/index.md` §14), and every such
row is re-examined in the P8 adversarial literature review. `unresolved` — open claim, no
literature search recorded, or attribution explicitly deferred to P8; the Notes give the reason. `n/a — audit record` — a verification
record rather than a result: it carries no novelty determination and is cited as evidence only.

**Columns.** Row · Item · v8 location (`§n Lx–y`, or `new section`) · Claim ids (registry id,
`KNOWN_RESULTS` label in parentheses) · Evidence (the registry command) · Novelty · Attribution
(author–year sources the statement rests on or must cite) · Notes (scope, hardened wording v9
must carry, what v9 must not say; ≤40 words).

**Summary.** 133 rows: direct corollary 45 · unresolved 38 · known 23 · adaptation 14 · apparently new 9 · audit records 4.

**How to use for v9.** Rows labelled `known` are cited, not claimed. Rows labelled `apparently
new` may be presented as contributions only with the conservative wording "we found no direct
precedent" and the attribution column cited as nearest prior art. Rows labelled `unresolved`
are presented as witnesses or diagnostics, never as novelty, until P8 closes them. The Notes
column overrides any v8 wording it contradicts.
