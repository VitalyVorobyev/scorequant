A partial assignment can be bounded above by completing it with one singleton cell per unassigned observation, because refining a partition can only increase information. On small instances this lets a bounded search either prove a partition optimal or report honestly that its budget ran out before it could. The resulting certificate always states which of the two happened; a certificate that reports the budget as exhausted proves nothing about the incumbent partition, and says so explicitly.
