The supporting inequality behind the exchange-implies-Voronoi argument (`D-EXCHANGE-IMPLIES-VORONOI`) is a standard projection-leverage bound: for every centroid, a quadratic form against the inverse information matrix is bounded by the reciprocal of the cell's weight, and an analogous bound holds for the difference between two centroids. This is a known result rather than a contribution.
