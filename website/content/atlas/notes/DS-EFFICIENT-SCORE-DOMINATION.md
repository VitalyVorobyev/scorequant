entity: DS-EFFICIENT-SCORE-DOMINATION
kind: claim
---
For any rule, the profiled information it retains is dominated by the between-cell information of the full-data efficient score under that same rule. Maximizing the latter over all rules with the same number of cells therefore gives a computable ceiling for the former — a number that can be subtracted from an achieved objective to see how much room is left — and the maximizer also supplies a strong initializer for the search.

The variational identity underneath this domination bound is re-attributed to Krein, and to Anderson, and to Li and Mathias, and its statistical reading is textbook semiparametrics. This is the highest-risk attribution among the results discussed here, and it is treated as a corollary of known variational and semiparametric theory rather than as a contribution.
