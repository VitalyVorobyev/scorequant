At an exchange-stable profiled partition there is an exact inequality relating the leverages of the two cells involved in any candidate move, and it needs no assumption that the cells carry balanced mass. This is the technical ingredient that substitutes for the exact Voronoi geometry the profiled case does not have.

No direct precedent was found in a targeted search; the classical D-side leverage inequality (`D-LEVERAGE`) is the nearest cousin, and it remains possible that a closer prior statement exists for the profiled case.
