For the profiled objective — some parameters of interest, the rest nuisance — the argument that works for the full determinant criterion fails at its first step: a positive first-order margin in the natural profiled semimetric does not imply a positive exact gain, so exchange stability does not force nearest-cell assignment. This is the finite efficient-semimetric gap that the profiled case has in place of a geometry theorem.
