When only some parameters are of interest and the rest are treated as nuisance, the profiled information objective is the Schur complement of the full information matrix, and its log determinant equals the log determinant of the full information minus the log determinant of the nuisance block.

This is classical design theory, due to Näther and Reinsch, who developed the profiled case within optimal experimental design; it is cited here and not claimed.
