For conditionally centred score laws, and under one further regularity condition on the law, all sufficiently large samples admit no ordinary exchange-stable labelling carrying the mass, conditioning and centroid-separation margins at once. This is a statement about population conditional centring — it is not permission to centre a sample, and it does not extend to non-centred laws.
