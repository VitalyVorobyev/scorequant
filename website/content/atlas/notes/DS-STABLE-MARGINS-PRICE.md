For every conditioning threshold there is a strictly positive gap between the best margin-carrying profiled value and the unconstrained one, asymptotically, and uniformly over every labelling rather than only over stable or optimal ones. The result neither asserts that the constrained optimum is attained nor that the price varies continuously with the threshold, and both of those omissions are explicit rather than implicit gaps.
