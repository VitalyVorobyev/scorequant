For the smallest-eigenvalue criterion, a positive first-order margin can come with a negative exact gain — the same kind of failure documented for the determinant criterion's profiled case, but here affecting the base criterion itself rather than only a profiled version of it. As with `E-GLOBAL-GEOMETRY-FAILS`, no prior-art search is recorded for this example.
