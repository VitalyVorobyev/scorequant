If each observation is replaced by its category label, the label carries its own likelihood, and the Fisher information it carries is the variance of the conditional mean of the score given the label. It is a sum over cells of one outer product each — the cell's weighted score sum against itself, divided by the cell's weight — with no approximation and no asymptotics involved.

The closest theoretical predecessor is Barnes, Han and Özgür, who characterized quantized Fisher information geometrically in terms of conditional score means and solved the one-bit Gaussian location problem exactly; the retained-information identity here is attributed to that line of work.
