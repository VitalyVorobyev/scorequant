Consistency is proved only for a restricted class of rules (`CONSISTENCY-RESTRICTED-AFFINE`). Whether unrestricted empirical global optima converge, in value or in decision, to population optima is open. The structural theorem makes the determinant case look favourable — global optima are geometrically realizable, so the unrestricted and restricted problems have the same optimal value — but that observation falls short of a proof.
