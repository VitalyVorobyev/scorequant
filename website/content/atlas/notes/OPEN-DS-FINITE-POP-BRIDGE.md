entity: OPEN-DS-FINITE-POP-BRIDGE
kind: claim
---
Restricting attention to partitions that carry margins — cells with enough mass, a binned information matrix bounded away from singular, centres that stay separated — recovers a finite-to-population bridge: one-point exchange-stable finite profiled labellings satisfying those margins are asymptotically geometric. This is a conditional theorem whose hypotheses are assumed rather than derived from more primitive conditions.
