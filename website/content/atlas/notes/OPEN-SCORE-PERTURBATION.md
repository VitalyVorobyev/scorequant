There is no perturbation theory bounding the change in cell moments, objective value and cell boundaries from a norm bound on the score estimation error. This absence, together with the missing calibration-to-information propagation (`OPEN-CLASSIFIER-CALIBRATION-FI`), is why estimated-score provenance never claims exact Fisher semantics anywhere in the library — the downgrade reflects the absence of a theorem, not excess caution.
