Every theorem in this area is a statement about a table of numbers; it is silent about where those numbers came from. When the score is estimated — from a classifier, a learned ratio, a simulator — the information actually retained is governed by the true score conditioned on the estimated rule, not by the estimated score conditioned on itself. The two coincide only when the proxy is exact in the relevant sense; this is why estimated-score provenance never claims exact Fisher semantics.
