entity: SOFT-FIXED-TEMP-STATIONARY
kind: claim
---
Optimizing a soft, temperature-controlled assignment at a fixed positive temperature has the ordinary guarantees of smooth nonconvex optimization, and no others: a stationary point of the soft objective is not thereby a local or global optimum of the hard objective it approximates. Hardening a soft fit is therefore a step whose result must be checked against the hard objective rather than assumed.
